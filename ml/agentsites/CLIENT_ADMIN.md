> Your guide to managing your website through Claude.
> No technical knowledge required.

## What This Is

Your website is managed through a conversation with Claude — an AI assistant.
Instead of logging into a website builder or CMS, you simply describe what
you want and Claude handles it.

This document tells you:
- How to talk to Claude to get things done
- What you can ask for
- What to expect
- When to contact Mack & Lee instead

---

## Getting Started

1. Go to claude.ai and open your **Sustained Outcomes** project
2. Everything Claude needs to know about your site is already loaded
3. Just start talking — describe what you want in plain English

## What You Can Ask Claude To Do

### Blog Posts

**New post:**
> "Write a blog post about our first Oakland Outdoors meetup at Stony Creek
> Metropark last Saturday. About 12 families showed up, kids aged 8-14.
> We did a nature scavenger hunt and two parents asked about volunteering.
> Category: Connecting with Nature."

**With an image:**
> "Use this photo for the hero image: [paste URL]"

**With a video:**
> "Embed this YouTube video in the post: [paste YouTube URL]"

**Schedule a post:**
> "Write this post but don't publish it until June 1st."

**Edit an existing post:**
> "In my post about the Erb Fellowship meeting, change the second
> paragraph to say [new text]."

---

### Landing Pages

**New landing page:**
> "Create a registration page for Oakland Outdoors Summer Program.
> It runs June 15 - August 30. Registration deadline is June 1.
> Link the button to this form: [URL].
> Use this image: [URL]."

**Update a landing page:**
> "Update the Oakland Outdoors page — change the deadline to June 15
> and update the hero image to this one: [URL]."

---

### General Content Updates

**Update page copy:**
> "On the About page, update my bio to include that I joined the
> Erb Foundation fellowship in January 2025."

**Update a button:**
> "Change the 'Request a Meeting' button to say 'Schedule a Call'."

**Add a partner logo:**
> "Add Oakland County Parks to the Partners page.
> Here's their logo: [URL]. Their website is oakgov.com/parks."

---

### Things Claude Will Ask You For

When creating anything new, Claude may ask:
- **Image:** "Do you have an image for this? Paste a URL or describe
  what you'd like."
- **Link:** "Where should this button go? A form, email, or another page?"
- **Category:** "Which focus area does this fall under — Access to Nature,
  Connecting with Nature, or Business Sustainability?"

Just answer in plain English. Claude will handle the rest.

---

## What to Expect After You Ask

1. Claude will confirm what it's going to do before doing it
2. Claude will make the change and give you a **preview link**
3. Review the preview — does it look right?
4. Tell Claude "looks good" and it will publish it live
5. Changes are usually live within 60 seconds of publishing

If something doesn't look right, just say so:
> "The headline is too long, shorten it."
> "The image isn't showing — try this URL instead: [URL]"

## Images & Media

**For blog posts and landing pages:**
- Upload your photo anywhere public — Google Photos, Dropbox,
  your phone's shared link, Flickr
- Share the public link with Claude
- Claude will embed it correctly

**For video:**
- Upload to YouTube or Vimeo as you normally would
- Share the video link with Claude
- Claude will embed it in the page

**Image tips:**
- Landscape orientation works best for hero images
- Minimum 1200px wide for good quality
- Your nature photography is perfect as-is

## Your Monthly Report

On the 1st of each month you'll receive an email summary of:
- How many people visited your site
- Which pages and blog posts were most popular
- How many people clicked "Request a Meeting"
- What's working and what to focus on next month

No login required — it comes straight to your inbox.
If you have questions about the report, ask Claude:
> "Can you explain what the traffic numbers mean this month?"

## What's Off Limits for Claude

Some things require Mack & Lee to handle directly.
Contact them for:

- Changing the overall design or layout
- Adding a completely new section type to the homepage
- Changing colors, fonts, or branding
- Setting up a new form or payment system
- Domain or hosting issues
- Anything that looks broken site-wide

When in doubt, describe what you want to Claude first.
If it's outside Claude's scope, Claude will tell you
and recommend contacting Mack & Lee.

## Tips for Best Results

**Be specific about what you want:**
> ✅ "Write a 300-word blog post about the Erb Foundation fellowship
>     kickoff meeting. Tone: professional but approachable."
> ❌ "Write something about the fellowship."

**Share context:**
> ✅ "Here are my notes from the meeting: [paste notes]"
> ❌ "You know what happened, write about it."

**One thing at a time:**
> ✅ "Create a landing page for the summer program."
> Then: "Now add a deadline countdown to it."
> ❌ "Create a landing page, add a countdown, link it to the form,
>     and also update the homepage hero."

**If Claude gets something wrong:**
> Just tell it: "That's not quite right — [explain what to change]."
> Claude will fix it. You don't need to start over.
